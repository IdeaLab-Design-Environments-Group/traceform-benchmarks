**`bat-wing-left.stl`** — In-project asset of the kiri project; the left wing of the same bat model. **The only mesh in that project that is not convex**, so the only one carrying valley folds. Vendored as the non-convex reference used by the test suite.

## Why convexity matters

A convex solid has no reflex edges, so after unfolding **every crease is a
mountain**.  Copper on the outer face is then in tension at every crossing,
`compression_crossing_count` is zero in every row, and the
tension-versus-compression half of the claim cannot be exercised.  The three
convex models still test whether a *graded* strain price beats a *flat*
mountain penalty; only `bat-wing-left.stl` can test the sign half.

## Footprints

The `.kicad_mod` files in `data/footprints/` are copied from the vendored
KiCad FabLib at `~/Documents/kiri/footprints/fab/`.  Pad positions are parsed
from them; no pad coordinate in this repository was typed by hand.

The FabLib carries no 1206 Schottky, so the Schottky uses the `R_1206` land
pattern -- a 1206 diode sits on exactly the two-pad land pattern a 1206
resistor does.  This is recorded in `src/layouts.py > PARTS`.
